# Explore the battlefield

This short tour introduces the scale changes, movement, and physical die. Start the application using the [README setup steps](../../README.md#run-locally). You need a keyboard and mouse.

## Step onto the table

Click **Enter the battlefield**. Move the mouse to look around and use W/A/S/D to move. You begin as the Primaris body near a short edge of the board. Look past the battlefield: the lamps, furniture, and room belong to a much larger world.

## Change your point of view

Stand beside a ruin and press **1**, then **2**, then **3**. The viewpoint changes height and the HUD names the selected body. Compare the same opening from each height. Narrow spaces can admit a smaller body while blocking a larger one.

## Jump and launch

Find a clear stretch of mat. Tap **Space** for a normal jump. Hold **Shift** while moving to sprint.

Select **3**, land, face along the clear stretch, and hold **Space**. A normal jump begins immediately; after a short hold the pack launches you forward. You can look around during the flight, but movement keys cannot steer it. The pack brakes as you approach a surface below.

If you overshoot the table, wait for the fall to return you to the start.

## Roll a die

Press **R** and watch the die enter the table, bounce, and settle. The HUD displays the detected upward face. Press **R** again to replace it: the previous die rises and shrinks away. A cocked or off-table result triggers an automatic reroll.

The dice system is experimental. If the displayed result disagrees with the visible face, that is a defect to investigate, not an expected part of the tour.

## Adjust your view and leave

Press **Esc**, choose **Options**, and adjust mouse sensitivity. Go **Back**, then **Resume**. The setting survives a page reload at the same site address.

When finished, close the browser tab and press **Ctrl+C** in the server terminal. For the complete input list, see [controls](../../README.md#controls).
